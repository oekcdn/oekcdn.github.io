The History of UPOTSS
-by @oekoff, last commited 19-11-2026 0:06am

*The Beginning (Before the Community)*

Long before the community was established, the foundation began with a group simply known as “A Group of Random People.” This was the first group I joined. Out of curiosity, I asked my little sister what the group was about and requested to be added.
When I joined, the group had around four admins. Most of the admins were Australians, with only one Malaysian admin. During its peak, the group reached 1,022 members before its member count eventually declined.
After some time, I decided to invite my real-life friend, Cleverley, into the group. I also brought in several original members who would later become important figures in the community: Amirul, Aiden, Hector, and Fabriozzy.
As time passed, the group began growing again. Members started sharing invitations to other groups, causing the community surrounding it to expand.


*The First Admin Expansion*

Eventually, the head admin decided to promote me as an admin. After becoming an admin, I created a poll to decide who should become the next admins. The poll had four candidates (the exact entries may vary since this happened months ago, around December 2025):
- Cleverley
- Carmen
- Pokemon Guy (originally named “.” or “Dot”)
- Ratatu
Instead of choosing only one, I decided to promote all of them because they were trusted and contributed positively to the group.


*The First Major Crisis _(January 2026)_*

In January 2026, disaster struck. The head admin and almost all other admins left the group, leaving only four admins and Kirky as the remaining leadership of A Group of Random People.
This became one of the first major turning points in the group’s history.


*The First Community Attempt _(February–March 2026)_*

Around February 2026, I created the first community. The goal was to build a large-scale community capable of supporting up to 51,000 members.
The community initially contained 14 hub groups.
However, as months passed, activity started declining. By March 2026, many groups had become inactive, so I decided to leave the community. Eventually, the community slowly died.
The original Hub 1 group was renamed “Random People Chat” and was removed from the community.


*The Second Community Era*

About two weeks later, Frost created a new community and added:
A Group of Random People
Everyone Group (owner: Natrium)
This marked the beginning of a new phase.


*The John Noah Incident _(April 2026)_*

In April 2026, the John Noah incident occurred.
John Noah somehow gained control of the community, becoming the first recorded case of a community hijacking in its history.
Because of this incident, the decision was made to abandon the compromised community.
A new community was created with the [NEW] prefix, and ownership was transferred to Cleverley.
All lost groups were restored and added back into the new community.


*Minecraft Era _(May 2026)_*

In May 2026, the community launched its first Minecraft server:
Vzanarchy
It was an anarchy Minecraft server owned by Ok05.
The server operated for around 1.2 months before temporarily shutting down.
A week later, it returned alongside a second server:
VZSMP
This gave the community two Minecraft servers.
However, after another week, both servers were forced to close again due to hosting issues.


*The Second Hijacking _(June 2026)_*

In June 2026, another major event happened.
The original A Group of Random People group, considered the foundation and legacy of the entire community, was hijacked by five individuals.
After investigation:
Three people pleaded guilty.
One person was accused.
One person denied involvement.
This became another significant event in the community’s history.


*The Website Era _(June–July 2026)_*

Around June 2026, the community launched its first official website:
fxmeet.github.io
The website was created as an introduction and information page for the community.
However, nine days later, the website was taken down by GitHub.
Around two weeks before the end of June 2026, a replacement website was created:
vazenetwork.github.io
This became the new official website, continuing the purpose of the previous one.
